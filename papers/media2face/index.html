SIGGRAPH Conference Papers · 2024

Media2Face: Co-speech Facial Animation Generation With Multi-Modality Guidance

Qingcheng Zhao, Pengyu Long, Qixuan Zhang, Dafei Qin, Han Liang, Longwen Zhang, Yingliang Zhang, Jingyi Yu, Lan Xu

Research overview

Media2Face creates expressive 3D facial animation guided by speech and additional text or image cues. A generalized facial representation separates identity from expression and helps extract motion from videos to build a diverse, annotated training dataset. A diffusion model then generates facial motion in that representation, allowing variations in emotion and style alongside audio-driven performance.
