ChatAvatar: Creating Hyper-realistic Physically-based 3D Facial Assets through AI-Driven Conversations

Qixuan Zhang, Longwen Zhang, Lan Xu, Di Wu, Jingyi Yu

Research overview

ChatAvatar creates detailed 3D facial assets through conversational text input. Diffusion-based generation and a dataset of production-ready faces support the creation of geometry and physically based materials. The workflow makes character creation accessible through natural-language descriptions while producing assets intended for conventional computer-graphics pipelines.
