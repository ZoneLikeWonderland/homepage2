DreamFace: Progressive Generation of Animatable 3D Faces under Text Guidance

Longwen Zhang, Qiwei Qiu, Hongyang Lin, Qixuan Zhang, Cheng Shi, Wei Yang, Ye Shi, Sibei Yang, Lan Xu, Jingyi Yu

Research overview

DreamFace turns written descriptions into personalized 3D faces with geometry, materials, and animation controls. A progressive process refines facial shape and synthesizes appearance using diffusion models. The assets share a topology compatible with established graphics tools, while learned expression priors add person-specific deformation for animating the generated characters.
