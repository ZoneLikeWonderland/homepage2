Strips as Tokens: Artist Mesh Generation with Native UV Segmentation

Rui Xu, Dafei Qin, Kaichun Qiao, Qiujie Dong, Huaijin Pi, Qixuan Zhang, Longwen Zhang, Lan Xu, Jingyi Yu, Wenping Wang, Taku Komura

Research overview

SATO generates artist-style meshes with a sequence representation based on triangle strips. Connected faces and explicit UV boundaries help preserve coherent edge flow and meaningful surface partitions. The same representation can produce triangular or quadrilateral meshes, allowing training to combine large triangle datasets with more structured quad examples.
